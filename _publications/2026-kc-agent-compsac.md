KC-Agent is a dual-process cognitive architecture for efficient ML model improvement, developed in the research line on cognitive architectures for agents at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center & Lenovo. It builds on the CAMA framework to enable agents that continuously adapt and improve in real-world environments.
