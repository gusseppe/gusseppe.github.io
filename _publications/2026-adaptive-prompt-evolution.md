Autonomous prompt evolution for LLM-based agents in privacy-sensitive healthcare applications, achieving **99% storage reduction** while maintaining **96.9% accuracy** across diverse patient populations. This work extends the continual learning line of research with a focus on resource-efficient agent adaptation.
