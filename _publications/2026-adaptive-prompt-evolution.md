Adaptive Prompt Evolution (APE) enables autonomous prompt evolution for continual learning in privacy-sensitive healthcare applications, achieving **99% storage reduction** while maintaining **96.9% accuracy** across diverse patient populations. This work extends the TADIL research line with a focus on resource-efficient agent adaptation.
