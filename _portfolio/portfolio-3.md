**Publications:** ICPR 2024 (Oral), CVPR Workshop 2023

Task-agnostic domain-incremental learning approach using transformer nearest-centroid embeddings. Enables effective domain adaptation without requiring task labels, achieving 6% performance improvement across diverse domains.

- Selected for oral presentation at ICPR 2024
- Featured in ContinualAI invited talk
- Applied to autonomous driving scenarios
- Highlighted in Lenovo Press article

**Key Features:**
- Task-ID inference using transformer embeddings
- Domain adaptation without task labels
- Scalable to multiple domains
- Production-ready implementation
