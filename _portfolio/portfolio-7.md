**Context:** Developed at Emory Global Diabetes Research Center.

TADILER combines **experience replay and zero-shot clustering** with foundation model embeddings for strategy-agnostic domain incremental learning:

- Shortlisted for the **Best Student Paper Award at VISAPP 2025**
- Improves accuracy up to **3.1%** on diverse patient populations
- **96.9% accuracy with 99% storage reduction** in privacy-sensitive settings

**Open source:** [tadiler](https://github.com/gusseppe/tadiler)  
**Publications:** [VISAPP 2025](https://doi.org/10.5220/0013128600003912)
